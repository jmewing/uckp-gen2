**2026-10-07 ~03:40 CDT** — the UCK G2 Plus boots a mainline **6.19** kernel to a full
Debian userspace **with working Ethernet**. This closes the port.

## The three fixes that took it from "dark box" to "up with network"

### Fix 1 — console (v12)
Stock cmdline `console=ttyHSL0` never binds in mainline (`msm_serial` registers
**`ttyMSM0`**). Every 6.19 boot was silent. → `console=ttyMSM0,115200n8`.

### Fix 2 — the pre-mountroot hang (v16)
Ubiquiti's initramfs `/scripts/ubnt-init/boot-aarch64` detects the board from
`/proc/cpuinfo`:
```sh
HARDWARE=$(sed -n '/^Hardware/p' /proc/cpuinfo)
case "${HARDWARE}" in *APQ8053) HWID=apq8053 ;; esac
. /scripts/ubnt-init/${HWID}/boot-${BOOTMODE}
```
**Mainline arm64 removed the `Hardware:` line from `/proc/cpuinfo`** → `HWID=unknown`
→ sources the non-existent `unknown/boot-emmc` → boot dies before `mountroot()`.
→ fall back to the DT model string (`/sys/firmware/devicetree/base/model`).

### Fix 3 — USB3 SuperSpeed PHY (v18) ← the network
The UCK has **no on-SoC Ethernet**; LAN is the ASIX **AX88179** behind a TI USB3 hub
on the SuperSpeed path. Mainline msm8953 has **no SuperSpeed PHY** (only the HS
qusb2 PHY) and caps the controller at `high-speed`. Ported it:

1. **Kernel config** — `CONFIG_PHY_QCOM_QMP=y`, `CONFIG_PHY_QCOM_QMP_USB=y`
   (must be **built-in**; the vendor ramdisk loads no 6.19 modules).
2. **gcc-msm8953** — gave `gcc_usb3_pipe_clk` its PHY parent
   (`usb3_phy_pipe_clk_src`), matching the msm8996 pattern
   (`kernel-port/gcc-msm8953-usb3-pipe-clk.patch`).
3. **DTS** — added the QMP SS PHY node at `0x78000` (mainline
   `qcom,msm8996-qmp-usb3-phy`, QMP v3) with clocks `aux/cfg_ahb/ref/pipe`, resets
   `phy_phy`/`phy`, regs `vdda-phy`=`pm8953_l3`, `vdda-pll`=`pm8953_l7`; wired
   `phys = <&hsusb_phy>, <&ssphy>; phy-names = "usb2-phy","usb3-phy";` on `&usb3_dwc3`
   and dropped `maximum-speed = "high-speed"`.

## Remaining polish (not blocking)

- Panel/OLED (`fb0`) not yet ported — cosmetic (`ck-splash` prints fb0 errors).
- `dphys-swapfile`, `exim4` units fail (carried over from stock rootfs).
- Optional: bring the NIC up at SuperSpeed (currently enumerates/links at HS).

## Healer note

The systemd `uck-bootcheck.service` (self-check/restore-to-stock) is **disabled** now
that 6.19 is the target — it must NOT restore stock on a good boot. It remains
available as a safety net by re-enabling. (Its `-b`→`-f` bug was fixed in v17; see
`6.19-v16-userspace-BREAKTHROUGH.md`.)

## Method (hard-won)

- **Never flash a boot image without a hang-surviving capture path.** v2–v11 were blind
  loops. v12's SD-card console+dmesg capture ended that.
- **Don't touch `/init`** (v13 regression bricked the ramdisk). Instrument the vendor
  scripts (`/scripts/ubnt`, `boot-aarch64`) instead.
- The console is **UART** (`ttyHSL0`/`ttyMSM0` @ MMIO 0x78af000), **not USB** — the UCK
  has no USB device-mode port. The SD-card tee is the practical "serial console".
